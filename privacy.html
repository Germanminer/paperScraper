Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Uni Exam to Calendar handles information.

The Extension is designed to read specific content from a university webpage and optionally create events in a user’s Google Calendar. Your privacy matters, and the Extension is built to collect as little information as possible.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information Processed Locally

The Extension may access and process:

  • Assessment names
  • Dates and Times
  • Related scheduling information visible on the university webpage

This data is processed locally in your browser and is not stored on external servers controlled by us.

b) Google Account Information

If you choose to connect your Google account, the Extension may request permission to view and create events in your Google Calendar.

The Extension only uses the minimum scope necessary to create calendar events. We do not:

  • Access your emails or files
  • Access unrelated Google services

3. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to extract assessment schedule details and create calendar events (if authorized). We do not sell, rent, share, or distribute your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Extracted schedule information is processed locally. No personal data is stored on external servers operated by us. Any Google authorization tokens are stored securely using Chrome’s built-in extension storage.

5. Third-Party Services

The Extension integrates with the Google Calendar API. Use of Google services is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Permissions

The Extension may request the following Chrome permissions:

  • Access to specific university webpages: To read assessment data.
  • Access to Google Calendar: To enable calendar integration.
  • Storage: To save user preferences and settings locally.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Extension. However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last updated” date at the top of this document.
